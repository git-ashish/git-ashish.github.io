Next-Gen Technology Adoption Report

Broadridge researches how financial-services firms take on new technology, and one of those studies — Next-Gen Technology Adoption — was rebuilt as something you read in a browser rather than open as a document.

From report to page

The findings arrived as a piece of research, the kind that usually ships as a PDF and gets skimmed once. A web edition took its place, so the same material could be worked through at a reader's own pace.

Reading it in the browser

Built in D3, the front end lets someone follow the report's argument on screen instead of scrolling a fixed layout. The original PDF stays linked from the page for anyone who wants the document in hand.

Who it's for

Its subject is technology adoption across financial services — who is taking up what, and how quickly. The web version was aimed at that audience specifically, so the reading matches how the sector actually digests this kind of research.
